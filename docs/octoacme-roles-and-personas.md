# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
The Product Lead frames the problem, defines what success looks like, and ensures the roadmap aligns with customer and business value. They work closely with PMs, engineering, and stakeholders to prioritize decisions and manage trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Define and refine product strategy, roadmap priorities, and outcomes
- Translate customer and business needs into clear success criteria
- Partner with engineering and PM to sequence work and resolve cross-functional dependencies
- Review feature direction and validate value delivery during planning and release cycles

### Goals
- Maximize value delivered to customers and stakeholders
- Ensure alignment between outcomes, roadmap, and team execution
- Maintain a clear and evidence-driven prioritization model

### Typical Communication
- Product roadmap reviews and prioritization meetings
- Stakeholder briefings and customer feedback synthesis
- Alignment discussions with PMs, engineering managers, and design leads

---

## Engineering Manager / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
The Engineering Manager or Tech Lead owns technical execution, team capacity, and delivery quality across the engineering workstream. They translate priorities into actionable delivery plans and help resolve technical dependencies and bottlenecks.

### Responsibilities
- Organize sprint or iteration planning for engineering work
- Manage technical dependencies, staffing trade-offs, and delivery risks
- Support architecture, implementation quality, and engineering standards
- Coordinate with product and project leaders on feasibility, sequencing, and scope trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality technical execution with predictable throughput
- Resolve technical blockers early and reduce delivery friction
- Balance team capacity with roadmap and milestone commitments

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and dependency reviews
- Technical risk discussions and design sign-offs
- Weekly status updates with PMs and product stakeholders

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
The UX / Design Lead shapes user experience, interaction quality, and research-informed design choices. They partner with product and engineering to ensure solutions are usable, coherent, and aligned with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define user journeys, flows, and design direction for features
- Collaborate with product to validate customer needs and usability goals
- Partner with engineering to ensure design feasibility and implementation quality
- Contribute to design review, accessibility considerations, and iterative improvements

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and valuable user experiences
- Align product decisions with customer behavior and usability evidence
- Reduce rework by validating design early and often

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and product discovery sessions
- Feedback loops with product, engineering, and QA
- Usability findings and iteration planning discussions

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
The QA / Test Lead defines quality strategy, validation criteria, and testing standards. They help ensure features are release-ready, resilient, and aligned with acceptance criteria before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and quality gates for each milestone or release
- Validate acceptance criteria, regressions, and core workflows
- Collaborate with engineering and release owners to eliminate release risk
- Support automation and manual test coverage for critical user journeys

### Goals
- Reduce defects and operational risk before release
- Ensure customer-facing quality is measurable and consistent
- Improve confidence across planning, execution, and release activities

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and acceptance criteria validation
- Cross-functional updates with PM, engineering, and stakeholders

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Lead ensures appropriate controls, reviews, and standards are integrated into planning and delivery. They help teams identify privacy, security, and compliance requirements before they become blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Review work for security, privacy, and compliance risk
- Define required controls and review checkpoints for delivery activities
- Partner with engineering and product on risk mitigation and governance
- Support secure release practices and incident readiness where applicable

### Goals
- Reduce cybersecurity and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure policies and controls are embedded into day-to-day delivery
- Prevent late-stage issues that would delay or threaten release quality

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and risk assessments
- Compliance or privacy checkpoints during planning and release
- Escalation paths for security-related issues and remediation planning

---

## Release / Operations Lead

### Role Summary
The Release / Operations Lead coordinates deployment readiness, cutover planning, and operational support to reduce release risk. They partner with PMs, engineering, and support teams to ensure production readiness and smooth rollout.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, deployment plans, and rollback preparation
- Validate operational readiness, monitoring, and post-deploy verification
- Partner with support and stakeholders on communication before and after launch
- Help manage risk during production release and incident response handoff

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Ensure predictable and well-communicated deployment execution
- Support smooth transition from project delivery to ongoing operations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Post-deploy verification and stakeholder updates
- Operational coordination with support, engineering, and PM teams

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles create a fuller cross-functional operating model for project planning, execution, quality, governance, and release work.
